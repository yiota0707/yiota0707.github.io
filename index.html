Research

Questions that shape my academic interests.

I am interested in intelligent systems that learn from experience, interact with other agents and operate under uncertainty.

How can autonomous agents learn to cooperate when their individual objectives may conflict?

I am interested in the mechanisms that influence cooperation, partner selection, strategic behaviour and coordination between learning agents.

This includes reinforcement learning, social dilemmas, adaptive interaction and the emergence of collective behaviour from individual decisions.

Reinforcement Learning Multi-Agent Systems Cooperation

How should an intelligent system make decisions when information is incomplete or constantly changing?

I am interested in learning algorithms that estimate future consequences, adapt over time and support autonomous decision-making under uncertainty.

This includes reinforcement learning, simulation-based approaches and adaptive systems that improve through experience.

Decision-Making Simulation Autonomous Systems

How can machine learning help us understand and monitor complex physical systems?

I am interested in intelligent diagnostics, condition monitoring and predictive methods for noisy, incomplete and changing engineering data.

More broadly, I am interested in the use of artificial intelligence to support reliable real-world technical decisions.

Applied AI Diagnostics Engineering Data

How can mathematical and formal tools help us reason rigorously about computational systems?

My interests include type theory, proof assistants, formal verification and mathematical logic.

I am especially interested in situations where rigorous methods can improve the reliability and clarity of computational research.

Research collaboration

AIS-Based Vessel Movement Detection

This project focused on detecting vessel movements in defined maritime areas using Automatic Identification System data.

The work included algorithm implementation, testing and analysis under real-world data limitations such as noise, missing reports, irregular transmission and message constraints.

The research was completed within the Department of Electrical Engineering, Computer Engineering and Informatics at the Cyprus University of Technology.

Cyprus University of Technology · 2025

BSc dissertation

Synthetic Differential Geometry in Type Theory

The project investigated the formalisation of Synthetic Differential Geometry through Homotopy Type Theory and the Agda proof assistant.

It included verified mathematical definitions and proofs involving curvature, metrics, infinitesimal transformations and geometric structures.

The project combined formal mathematics, proof development and visual representations of spherical, planar and hyperbolic spaces.

University of Nottingham · 2024–2025

Artificial intelligence project

Multi-Agent Coordination in Space Exploration

This project developed a three-dimensional rover simulation in a Mars-inspired environment.

The simulation compared PPO, MADDPG and evolutionary approaches under navigation, terrain, energy and coordination constraints.

The project explored how multiple autonomous agents could coordinate while responding to environmental limitations and shared objectives.
