I am interested in situations in which intelligent agents learn, make decisions and interact within complex environments.

01

Cooperative Artificial Intelligence

Assortative Matching and Cooperation in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning

How can self-interested learning agents develop and sustain cooperative behaviour?

Multi-agent environments often contain agents whose objectives partly align but may also conflict. Each agent learns from its own experience, but its outcomes depend on the behaviour of others.

My current MSc dissertation investigates how assortative matching and partner-selection mechanisms influence cooperation among reinforcement learning agents in repeated social dilemmas.

The work compares baseline and hybrid multi-agent models using Q-learning, adaptive partner switching and assortative rematching.

Q-Learning Multi-Agent Systems Partner Selection Social Dilemmas Simulation
02

Maritime Data and Algorithm Development

AIS-Based Vessel Movement Detection

How can vessel movements be reliably identified from noisy and incomplete maritime data?

Automatic Identification System data contains positional and navigational information transmitted by maritime vessels.

During my research collaboration with the Cyprus University of Technology, I developed and tested algorithms for detecting vessel movement within maritime areas of interest.

The project examined practical limitations including reporting errors, irregular transmissions, missing observations and message constraints.

Python AIS Data Algorithm Development Data Analysis

Formal Methods

Synthetic Differential Geometry in Type Theory

How can mathematical structures be represented and verified using formal proof systems?

Formal methods allow mathematical definitions and proofs to be represented in a form that can be checked mechanically.

My undergraduate dissertation investigated Synthetic Differential Geometry through Homotopy Type Theory and the Agda proof assistant.

The work included mechanically verified definitions and proofs involving curvature, metrics, transformations and geometric structures.

Cooperative AI studies how intelligent agents can coordinate, develop trust and achieve outcomes that are beneficial beyond their own immediate objectives.

I am especially interested in partner selection, social dilemmas, strategic interaction and the emergence of cooperation between reinforcement learning agents.

Research Interest

Intelligent Decision-Making

Intelligent systems often need to act before every possible outcome can be evaluated.

I am interested in algorithms that learn from experience, estimate future consequences and adapt their decisions under uncertainty.

Engineering data is frequently noisy, incomplete and generated by systems whose behaviour changes over time.

MSc Dissertation Research

Assortative Matching and the Emergence of Cooperation in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Systems

This research investigates how matching and partner-selection mechanisms influence cooperation between reinforcement learning agents in repeated Prisoner’s Dilemma environments.

The work includes baseline and hybrid models, large-scale simulations, sensitivity analysis, ablation studies, convergence analysis, population experiments and behavioural evaluation.

Python Q-Learning Multi-Agent Systems Statistical Analysis

Research Collaboration

The project focused on implementing and testing algorithms for detecting vessel movement within maritime areas of interest.
