MSc Dissertation

University of Warwick 2025–Present

Assortative Matching and the Emergence of Cooperation in Multi-Agent Reinforcement Learning Systems

How can matching and partner-selection mechanisms encourage cooperation between autonomous learning agents?

This research investigates repeated Prisoner’s Dilemma environments in which reinforcement learning agents can adapt both their strategic behaviour and their interaction partners.

I developed baseline and hybrid Python models using Q-learning, partner switching and assortative rematching. The experimental programme includes sensitivity analyses, ablation studies, population studies, convergence analysis and behavioural evaluation.

Q-Learning Multi-Agent Systems Cooperation Experimental Design Statistical Analysis

Research Collaboration

Cyprus University of Technology 2025

AIS-Based Vessel Movement Detection

How can vessel movements be identified reliably from noisy, incomplete and irregular maritime data?

I developed and tested algorithms using Automatic Identification System data to detect vessel movement in maritime areas of interest.

The work considered practical limitations such as reporting errors, missing observations, irregular transmissions and message constraints.

Python AIS Data Algorithm Development Data Analysis

BSc Dissertation

University of Nottingham 2024–2025

A Study of Synthetic Differential Geometry in Type Theory

How can advanced mathematical structures be represented and verified within a proof assistant?

This work explored Synthetic Differential Geometry using Homotopy Type Theory, dependent type theory and the Agda proof assistant.

I developed mechanically verified definitions and proofs involving curvature, metrics, transformations and geometric structures.

Agda Type Theory Formal Proofs Mathematical Logic
